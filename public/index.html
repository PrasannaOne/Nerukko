About

Closer, together.

A small place for easy conversations. Join without an account and leave whenever you like.

How it works

Random groups and nearby rooms hold up to seven people. Location is the normal way Nerukko finds nearby people and rooms. It is used only for matching and discovery, never shown to other members. If permission is declined, random matching uses the rough city-level area of your connection instead.

Messages and privacy

Messages travel directly between browsers over encrypted WebRTC when a connection is available. The Worker coordinates rooms but does not store ordinary chat messages. Pending text stays in your browser until delivery or expiry. Accepted friends and up to 100 recent text messages per private friend are saved locally on this device.

Private chats and stickers

One accepted invitation moves two people into a separate one-to-one conversation. Nearby Rooms shows up to ten public and ten private rooms in your area. Rooms are fixed to their creation location and disappear when empty, immediately or after at most 24 hours. Images and GIFs are private-only, and photos are shrunk automatically before sending; lightweight stickers work in group, room and private chats. Stickers you create are saved on your device, and pending stickers wait locally until a person arrives.

Nerukko Bot

The Bot is AI, not a person. Bot messages and recent conversation context are sent to Google Gemini to generate replies. Replies may be imperfect and usage is capped.

Safety and connection

Block hides a person and their messages only from your own view. Two independent reports in the same room trigger a temporary cooldown: five minutes for the first incident, ten for the second, and fifteen for later incidents. STUN-only connections may not work on some restrictive networks.
