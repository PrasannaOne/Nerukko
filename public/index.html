A little about the place

About Nerukko

Closer, together.

A small corner for easy conversations. No account needed. Drop in, meet a few people and say hello.

How Nerukko works

Groups hold up to five people. Your browser keeps a random ID and nickname. Location is optional and used only for matching, never shown to other members.

Messages and privacy

Messages travel directly between browsers over encrypted WebRTC when connections allow. The Worker coordinates rooms; it does not store chat content. Pending offline text stays in the sender's browser until delivery or expiry.

Private chats and sharing

Private requests move two people into a one-to-one chat. Images and GIFs are private-only. Stickers work in groups and private chats. You can make a sticker from a photo on your device by cropping it, adding an optional white outline, and saving it locally. Recipients receive the image itself, not a pack dependency.

Nerukko Bot

When you are alone in a group, Nerukko Bot may join. It is AI, not a human. Bot messages and recent chat context are sent to Google Gemini; replies may be imperfect. Usage is capped.

Safety and connection

Two independent reports can trigger a five-minute cooldown. STUN-only connections may fail on restrictive networks.
