About Nerukko

Closer, together.
Meet people nearby in small, anonymous conversations. No account, no profile to build, no pressure. Just drop in and say hello.

Your browser keeps a random participant ID and nickname. The matchmaking server handles room presence and connection setup. Text and image messages are sent directly between browsers over encrypted WebRTC data channels when a peer connection is available. Nerukko does not store chat messages on its server.

Approximate location is optional and used only for matching. It is never shown to other members. Without location, we prioritise rooms with others who made the same choice.

Private conversations move both people out of the group. Images, GIFs and stickers are available only in private chats. The Black Cat sticker pack is bundled with Nerukko; recipients receive the selected sticker image directly and do not need the pack.

Nerukko Bot may appear when you are alone in a group. Messages in Bot conversations are sent to Google Gemini to generate replies. Chat content is not saved in Nerukko's server storage, but Google processes those prompts under its API terms.

Pending messages stay in the sender's browser and are sent directly when both people reconnect. Nerukko's server routes only the temporary connection setup, not the message text.

Two independent reports can trigger a five-minute cooldown. Direct connections may not work on every network because this launch uses STUN without a TURN relay.
