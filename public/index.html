About Nerukko

Closer, together.
Meet people nearby in small, anonymous conversations. No account, no profile to build, no pressure. Just drop in and say hello.

Your browser keeps a random participant ID and nickname. The matchmaking server handles room presence and connection setup. Text and image messages are sent directly between browsers over encrypted WebRTC data channels when a peer connection is available. Nerukko does not store chat messages on its server.

Approximate location is optional and used only for matching. It is never shown to other members. Without location, we prioritise rooms with others who made the same choice.

Private conversations move both people out of the group. Two independent reports can trigger a five-minute cooldown. Direct connections may not work on every network because this launch uses STUN without a TURN relay.
